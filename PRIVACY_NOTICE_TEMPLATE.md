## Information we use

We may use a company's name, company number, incorporation date, business sector, registered-office area, website address and a publicly displayed business contact email address.

We use this information to identify recently established UK corporate businesses that may reasonably be interested in practical AI education, community support and related Luminary AI services.

## Lawful basis

Where personal data is involved, we rely on legitimate interests for proportionate business-to-business marketing. We balance our interests against the rights and reasonable expectations of business contacts. We do not intentionally use personal consumer email addresses for this activity.

## Your choices and rights

You can object to marketing at any time by using the unsubscribe link in an email or contacting [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We will place the address on a suppression list so that it is not contacted again. You may also have rights of access, correction, restriction, objection and deletion under UK data-protection law.
